## ✦ About me

I am **Thais Oliveira**, a student of **Software Engineering** and **Systems Analysis and Development (ADS)** at **Universidade Ceuma**. I am at the beginning of my journey in the field of technology, studying **Python** and **HTML** through hands-on projects. Currently, I expect this academic background to provide me with a solid foundation in programming logic, data structures, algorithms, and databases, as well as in-depth knowledge of software design, testing, and maintenance.
